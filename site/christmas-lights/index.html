Questions

Do I buy the Christmas lights?

+

No. It works like a lease. One payment covers the lights, the installation, fixes all season, the takedown after New Year's and storage until next year.

What does $650 cover?

+

Our minimum: a single-story home with lights along the front roofline. Bushes, trees and more rooflines are priced in your estimate.

Do I need to be home for the takedown?

+

No. Between Christmas and New Year's we text you a date range, text again when we're on the way, and send photos when it's done.

Is it cheaper next year?

+

Yes. Returning customers get 10% off from the second year, and that stays your price.
