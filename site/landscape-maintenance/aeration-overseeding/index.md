## What aerating and seeding do.

1. ![Drawing of a lawn cut through the soil, with open channels and arrows showing air and water going down into them](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/drawn/aerate.svg) **Aerating opens the soil.** Channels let air and water into compacted ground.
2. ![Drawing of grass seed falling on the same lawn, landing on a bare patch and in the open channels](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/drawn/seed.svg) **Seed fills the thin spots.** Thicker grass crowds out weeds.
3. ![Drawing of water falling on the same lawn, with new blades in the bare patch and the top of the soil kept moist](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/drawn/water.svg) **Water brings it up.** Light and often, so the top inch of soil stays moist.

Source: University of Maryland Extension
