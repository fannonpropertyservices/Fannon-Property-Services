From the lawn to the planting beds

A real bed, from bare to planted

Beside a stone house, Fannon's before photo shows patchy grass around a lamp post. In the after photo, a dark mulch bed curves around it, with flowers and plants spaced through the new bed. That is a specific piece of landscape work, not a generic lawn image. Fannon also lists flowerbed installation and cleanup, shrub and plant installation, and mowing for the surrounding grass.

More than a mowing visit

A lawn can call for mowing, aeration and overseeding, or new sod. Fannon lists each of those as a separate part of landscape maintenance. Mulch installation and irrigation are also on the service list. A request can focus on grass, beds or both; the scope comes from the areas you want worked on, not a fixed menu applied to every yard.

The work changes with the season

Spring and fall cleanups sit beside the planting and lawn work, while snow removal is available when the season turns. That range is useful when the same property has different needs through the year. For an estimate, describe the lawn, beds and any seasonal work separately. Fannon gathers the details needed for a quote and arranges a walkthrough if the site needs a closer look.
