Privacy

What the forms on this site and on Facebook collect, what Fannon Property Services uses it for, and how to have your details deleted.

What happens to your details

You give us your name and number so we can text or call you about your property. Here is what our forms collect and where it goes.

What the forms collect

The estimate form on this site asks for your name, phone number, the property address and town, what you need done, and optionally your email and a note. Our Facebook and Instagram forms ask two questions about the job, followed by your name, phone number, email and city. Each request also records the page or ad it came from.

What we use it for

To text, call or email you about your estimate and the work on your property. We do not sell your details.

Calls and texts

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your request. Tell us to stop and we will.

Where it is kept

Requests are sent through Make to our email inbox and to a lead inbox that our marketing company runs for us. When we quote or schedule your job, your details go into Jobber, the software we use for that. If you used a Facebook or Instagram form, Meta also holds what you entered there under its own policy. This site is hosted by Netlify.

Other services this site uses

This site runs no analytics and no advertising trackers.

Deleting your details

Ask and we will delete your request: call or text (443) 223-8643, or email fannonpropertyservices@gmail.com.
