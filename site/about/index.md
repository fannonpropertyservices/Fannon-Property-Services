### Who runs Fannon Property Services?

Anthony Fannon owns the company and brings 5 years of experience to the work. We are locally owned, insured and based in Annapolis, Maryland. We look after lawns and planting beds, wash houses, clean gutters, fix yard drainage and put up Christmas lights across Anne Arundel County and on Kent Island.

## Questions

### What work do you do most?

Landscaping, all year: mowing, mulch, planting beds, sod, and spring and fall cleanups. Christmas light installation is our main seasonal service. We also wash houses and driveways, clean gutters, install gutter guards and fix yard drainage.

### Where are you based?

In Annapolis, Maryland. We work in Anne Arundel County, plus Stevensville and Chester on Kent Island, across the Bay Bridge. To reach us, call or text (443) 223-8643.

### Do you work on businesses as well as homes?

Yes, both. For a commercial property we look after the grounds, mulch and seasonal cleanups, exterior cleaning, snow and ice, holiday lighting and irrigation.
