Two gutter jobs, two decisions

First, the gutters themselves

Gutter cleaning is a distinct part of Fannon's exterior maintenance work. The portfolio photo shows an open gutter beneath a metal roof, with the channel visible along its length. If material is visible at the edge or water spills over during rain, those observations help describe the concern. They do not decide the scope on their own; Fannon can look at the roofline before quoting when needed.

Then, whether to add guards

Fannon also installs gutter guards. Cleaning and guards are two separate services: one removes material already in the gutter, while the other adds guards to the selected runs. A customer can ask about either one or both. The estimate should name the gutters involved and say whether guards are included, so a cleaning request does not silently become an installation request.

What happens around the roofline

Gutters are one piece of exterior care. Fannon also lists house washing and drainage solutions, but neither follows automatically from a gutter visit. If the concern is water collecting on the ground as well as the roof edge, mention both areas so they can be considered separately. For a quote, provide the property address and a way to reach you; a walkthrough can follow when the house needs a closer look.
