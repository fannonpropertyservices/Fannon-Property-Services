## Questions

### How steep is the ground in Herald Harbor?

Steep in places. Herald Harbor's largest land soil unit is Collington, Wist, and Westphalia soils on 25 to 40 percent slopes, mapped on 257.4 acres. Mention a bank when you ask for a price.

### Is Arden on the Severn any flatter?

Its main soil is. Arden on the Severn's largest soil unit is Sassafras fine sandy loam on 2 to 5 percent slopes, mapped on 157.3 acres.

### Where does Crownsville ground stay wet?

On low ground. 338.8 acres of the Crownsville Census place are mapped as frequently flooded, poorly drained Widewater and Issue soils. The largest streams and floodplains in the planning region are Bacon Ridge Branch and Broad Creek.

### Do you mow big Crownsville lots?

Yes. The county describes most Crownsville homes as sitting on large lots with a lot of privately held green space, and every lawn is quoted on its own.

### Should I mark my septic field before aeration?

Please do. Nearly all of the Crownsville planning region relies on septic systems, so flag the tank and the drain field lines for the crew.
