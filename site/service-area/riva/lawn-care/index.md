## Questions

### What soil are Riva lawns on?

Annapolis soil, in most places. Soil map units that include it cover about three quarters of Riva's land, and 86 percent of the land is rated well drained.

### Does any Riva ground stay wet?

Some low ground does. On about 4 percent of the census area lie Widewater and Issue soils, frequently flooded and poorly drained, with a water table as shallow as about 5 inches. Only about 9 percent of the land is on soils rated hydrologic group D or a dual D group.
