### Which towns does Fannon Property Services cover?

We work in Annapolis and across central and eastern Anne Arundel County: north to Pasadena around Lake Shore, Riviera Beach and Gibson Island, south to Riva, Edgewater and Londontowne, and west to Davidsonville, Crofton and Gambrills. Over the Bay Bridge we cover Stevensville and Chester on Kent Island. Severna Park, Arnold and Crownsville sit in the middle.

## Questions

### Do you come across the Bay Bridge?

Yes. Chester is as far east as we go. The far south end of Kent Island and the rest of the Eastern Shore are too far for us.

### How far west do you go?

To Davidsonville, as far as about Davidsonville Park, and to Crofton, which is one of the areas we work in most. Woodwardville and Academy Junction are covered too, and we do not go much farther north or west than Academy Junction.

### My town is not named. Should I still ask?

Yes. We also work in smaller places such as Parole, Highland Beach, Cape St. Claire, Arden on the Severn and Herald Harbor. Text your address to (443) 223-8643 and we will tell you if we come to you.
