Which towns does Fannon Property Services cover?

We cover central and eastern Anne Arundel County and Kent Island. Pasadena, Lake Shore and Riviera Beach are the north edge. Gibson Island, Stevensville and Chester are the east edge. Edgewater is the south edge, and Davidsonville, Crofton and Gambrills are the west edge. Annapolis, Arnold, Severna Park and Crownsville sit in the middle.

Where the line runs

North

Pasadena, around Lake Shore, is about as far north as we go. Riviera Beach is in.

East

Gibson Island is in. Across the Bay Bridge we work in Stevensville and in Chester, and Chester is the farthest east we go. The far south end of Kent Island and the rest of the Eastern Shore are too far for us.

South

Edgewater and Londontowne, down along the South River.

West

Davidsonville, out to about Davidsonville Park. Crofton is one of the areas we work in most, and Gambrills, Woodwardville and Academy Junction are inside the line with it. We do not go much farther north or west than Academy Junction.
