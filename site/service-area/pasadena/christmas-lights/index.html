Do you install Christmas lights in Pasadena?

Yes, in Pasadena and in Riviera Beach. The starting price is $650: one story, lights on the front roofline. There is nothing to buy, since the lights are leased, and one payment pays for the design, a timer, repairs while they are up, the takedown in the new year and storage until next Christmas.

Questions

Which Pasadena street hangs lights across the road?

+

In 2024 the homes along Westwood Manor Way hung lights high across the street, and a local guide called it Pasadena's own 34th Street. Those lights are the neighbors' own, not ours.

Where is Pasadena's official Christmas tree lit?

+

The Pasadena Business Association holds the lighting of Pasadena's official Christmas tree each year at Lakeshore Plaza on Mountain Road. We have no 2026 listing for it yet.

Does Santa visit the Riviera Beach fire company?

+

He has: the Riviera Beach Volunteer Fire Company on Fort Smallwood Road has hosted a pizza and movie night with Santa in December.

Do you hang lights on older homes in Riviera Beach?

+

Yes. Riviera Beach's median home was built in 1979, and its biggest building decade was the 1980s. Send the address and say what you want lit; the estimate is free.
