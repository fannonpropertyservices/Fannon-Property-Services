## Questions

### Do you work on Gibson Island?

Yes. It is a private community, so if we need to see the work before we price it, tell us how we get onto the island. Text or call (443) 223-8643 with the address and the job.

### Can you lay sod on a lot that runs down to the water?

We install sod, but beside tidal water a new lawn cannot be created inside the Buffer, the protected strip at least 100 feet wide. Tell us how far from the shoreline the sod would go. A lawn that already runs down to the water can still be mowed, and we mow those.
