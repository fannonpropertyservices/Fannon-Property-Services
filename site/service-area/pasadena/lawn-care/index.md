## Questions

### How deep is the water under Patapsco sand?

Usually well down. Patapsco soil is in the two largest soil map units on the Lake Shore peninsula, and the Patapsco sand has free water about 40 to 72 inches down for three to six months of the year.

### Part of our Lake Shore yard floods. Can you still mow it?

Yes. Point out the low part when you ask for a price. On low ground in Lake Shore the soil survey maps Zekiah and Issue soils, poorly drained and frequently flooded.

### Does a septic field change anything for aeration?

Show us where the tank and drain field are first. Much of Lake Shore has no public sewer, and the county health department lists Chelsea Beach and Lake Shore as septic problem areas.

### Do you mow lawns on Gibson Island?

Yes. Gibson Island is a private community of about 200 homes on roughly 1,000 acres, maintained by the Gibson Island Corporation. Include the address and how often you want mowing.
