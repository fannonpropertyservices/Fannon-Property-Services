Yes, on end and center units. Tell us how many stories the row has when you ask for your free estimate. Yes. Many homes on the island are in early 20th century styles, among them cottage style, French Eclectic, Spanish Eclectic and Tudor Revival, set in a landscape plan the Olmsted Brothers designed in 1921. Send the address and we will arrange a free estimate.
