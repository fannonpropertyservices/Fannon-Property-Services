## Edgewater, straight answers

### My Edgewater street floods at high tide. Will yard drainage help?

Not with the tide. County storm drain records list a job on Shore Drive in Edgewater for street flooding during high tide, received in July 2022 and completed in May 2023. That record is the county's, and it is about a street. River rock in a yard handles rain and roof water, and it cannot hold a tide back.

### My Edgewater yard is outside the flood zone. Why does it still hold water?

Because a flood map does not show where rain ponds on a lawn. About 7 percent of Edgewater's land lies in the A, AE or VE zones of FEMA's effective flood map, and about 92 percent lies outside every flood area the map draws. That counts land, not homes. A yard that stays wet outside those zones is a question of rain and ground.

### How much rain does it take to leave standing water in Edgewater?

Less than a record storm. Standing water forced the closure of Pike Ridge Road in Edgewater on September 6, 2011, when a rain gauge near there recorded 2.29 inches. A road is not a lawn, but if yours ponds after an ordinary rain, tell us how deep it gets and how many days it lasts.

### Runoff is cutting a gully down my bank toward the water. Is that a drainage job?

It may be more than a yard job. On a steep part of the Historic London Town grounds, a 2023 county presentation describes stormwater runoff gathering into a channel and discharging into tidal water at high speed, with head cut erosion developing upland. Describe your bank and we will tell you whether river rock suits it or whether it needs a walkthrough first.
