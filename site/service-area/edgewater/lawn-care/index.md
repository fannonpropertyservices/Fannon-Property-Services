### Is the Critical Area Buffer wider on a sloped Edgewater lot?

It can be. Where slopes of 15 percent or more adjoin the 100 foot Buffer, the county expands it by four feet for every 1 percent of slope, or to 50 feet past the top of the slope, whichever is greater.
