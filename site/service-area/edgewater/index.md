## Questions

### Can you build a planting bed close to the shoreline?

We build beds and plant them, but close to tidal water Maryland's Critical Area rules apply. Inside the Buffer, a strip at least 100 feet wide, an existing lawn can still be mowed, but removing other plants needs an approved Buffer Management Plan. Tell us how far from the water the bed would be. Anne Arundel County's planning and zoning office has the Critical Area maps.

### My address says Edgewater but I am past Londontowne. Do you come out?
