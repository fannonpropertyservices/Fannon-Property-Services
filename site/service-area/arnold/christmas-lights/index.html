Questions

When is the Deep Creek lighted boat parade?

+

Deep Creek neighbors organize it and set the date, and we have no 2026 date to give. In 2024 it was the first of the Bay's lighted boat parades.

Where do people watch the Deep Creek parade?

+

In 2024 the boats were cheered from waterfront homes, a community beach and Donnelly's Dockside, a waterfront restaurant with boat slips at 1050 Deep Creek Avenue, on the Magothy River.

Can you light the water side of a house on the creek?

+

Yes. Tell us which sides you want lit, and the estimate prices every roofline you ask for. The $650 start covers a single-story front roofline only.

Do you put holiday lights on businesses in Arnold?

+

Yes, and each building gets a price of its own. The Arnold Village Center runs along both sides of Ritchie Highway from Old Frederick Road and Baltimore Annapolis Boulevard to Admirals Ridge Drive, with shopping centers, dining, a fire station, a post office and the Arnold Senior Center.
