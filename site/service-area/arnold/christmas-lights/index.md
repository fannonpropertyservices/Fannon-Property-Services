## Questions

### When is the Deep Creek lighted boat parade?

Deep Creek neighbors organize it and set the date, and we have no 2026 date to give. In 2024 it was the first of the Bay's lighted boat parades.

### Where do people watch the Deep Creek parade?

In 2024 the boats were cheered from waterfront homes, a community beach and Donnelly's Dockside, a waterfront restaurant with boat slips at 1050 Deep Creek Avenue, on the Magothy River.
