## Questions

### Why are tuliptrees so common around Chase Creek?

Much of the Chase Creek watershed was cleared long ago for tobacco and later for homes; perhaps half is still wooded. Past logging left tuliptrees more common on many sites.

### Do Arnold townhouses need their gutters cleaned too?

Yes, the same as a house does. Of Arnold's 9,102 homes, 6,359 are detached houses and 2,136 are attached homes such as townhouses, and we clean the gutters on both.

### Are most Arnold homes older?

Many are. The Census estimate counts only 468 Arnold homes from 2010 or later. The age of a gutter matters less than the trees over it.
