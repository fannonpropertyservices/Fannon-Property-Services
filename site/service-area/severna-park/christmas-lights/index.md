## Questions

### When is the Severna Park tree lighting?

The Severna Park chamber of commerce holds it every year at 1 Holly Avenue and sets its own date, so look for the chamber's notice. It is the town's event, not ours: we light private homes.

### Can you light a house on one of the creeks off the Severn?

Yes. On its Severn River side, Severna Park takes in Yantz Creek, Forked Creek, Stevens Creek, Sullivan Cove and Rock Cove. Tell us which sides of the house you want lit, the one facing the water included, and the estimate prices each.

### Can a shop on Ritchie Highway get holiday lights?

Yes. We light businesses as well as homes, and a business gets its own quote. The county's Region 4 Plan names Ritchie Highway in Severna Park as one of the region's major commercial nodes.
