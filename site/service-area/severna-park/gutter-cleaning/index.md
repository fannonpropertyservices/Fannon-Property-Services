About half of it is. Tree canopy covers 49.8 percent of Severna Park's land, by our average of the 2021 national tree canopy data, so a lot of roofs sit under branches that drop their leaves into the gutters each fall.

Not many. The Census estimate counts 447 attached homes such as townhouses among Severna Park's 14,215 homes. We clean the gutters on those as well as on the houses.
