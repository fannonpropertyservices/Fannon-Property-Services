## Questions

### What kind of soil do Severna Park lawns grow on?

Sandy in much of town. The soil mapped most often across Severna Park is the Patapsco-Fort Mott-Urban land complex, which USDA rates somewhat excessively drained.

### Are there wet spots in Severna Park lawns too?

Tell us if yours has one. On its Magothy River side the town takes in Cypress Creek, North Cypress Creek, Cattail Creek and Dividing Creek. If part of the yard stays soft after rain, we come and look before we price the mowing.

### Why do some Severna Park lawns dry out fast?

The soil mapped most often there is sandy: the top layer of Patapsco soil is about 92 percent sand by USDA's figure, and parts of Severna Park are mapped as Evesboro and Galestown soils, which USDA rates excessively drained.
