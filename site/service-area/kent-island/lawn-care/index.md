## Questions

### What soil do Kent Island lawns grow on?

Mostly silt loam. Mattapex-Butlertown silt loam is the most common soil in both towns: 30.4 percent of Stevensville's mapped land and 37.1 percent of Chester's.

### How much of Chester and Stevensville is farmland?

Cultivated crops cover 28.2 percent of Chester and 23.0 percent of Stevensville in the 2021 land cover data.

### When does the growing season run on Kent Island?

Between freezes, from about March 29 to November 12. Those are the median last spring freeze and first fall freeze at the NOAA station in Stevensville.
