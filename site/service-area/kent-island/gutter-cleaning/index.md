## Questions

### Can you clean out our gutters after a storm in Stevensville or Chester?

Yes, we clear the leaves and debris out of the gutters; fallen trees are not work we take on. Storms do bring big trees down there: in July 2010 one dropped a linden more than 100 years old onto the Kent Island Federation of Art building in Stevensville.

### How wooded are Stevensville and Chester?

Trees cover 30.4 percent of Stevensville's land and 23.5 percent of Chester's, by our average of the 2021 national tree canopy data, and woody wetlands take up 15.7 percent of Stevensville. Where a house backs onto the woods, its gutters catch what falls.

### Our Chester home is fairly new. Does it need gutter cleaning?

Yes. Leaves settle in a new gutter just as they do in an old one. An estimated 564 of Chester's 2,591 homes were built in 2010 or later, by the Census count.
