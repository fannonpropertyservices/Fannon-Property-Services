## Questions

### Is the ground in Gambrills different from Crofton's?

In places. In Gambrills, the sandy Patapsco-Evesboro-Fort Mott complex is mapped on 483.2 acres and rated somewhat excessively drained, while Collington-Wist-Urban land leads in Crofton.

### Do you mow lawns around Davidsonville?

Yes. Points on the USDA soil map around Davidsonville fall on Marr, Dodon and Howell soils in hydrologic group C, two of them on 15 to 25 percent slopes, so mention any steep ground when you ask for a price.

### When should Crofton lawns be aerated and seeded?

In late summer and early fall. University of Maryland Extension puts aerating and overseeding a tall fescue lawn in mid-August to mid-October, with seed down by mid-October. We take it on by itself or along with mowing.
