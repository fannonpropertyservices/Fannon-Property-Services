## Questions

### Do you need to see the property before giving a price?

Not always. We first get the details from you by text or phone, and some jobs can be priced from that. If yours has to be seen before we can price it, we set a time to meet you there for a walkthrough.

### Can I just text you the job?

Yes. Text (443) 223-8643 with the property address and what you would like done. It is the same number you would call, and we answer by text or by phone.

### Can a Christmas lights estimate show me a few options?

Yes. Tell us what you want lit, and your estimate can lay out a few options at different prices.
