Yard care guides for Maryland homes.

When to seed, how deep to mulch, how often to clean gutters, and what to do when water sits.

For the lawn.

  • When to aerate and overseed

    Mid-August to mid-October, for tall fescue.

  • Maryland lawn calendar

    Mowing can run March to late November.

  • Fall yard checklist

    Seed, gutters, leaves, a last mow.

Wet ground, gutters and beds.

  • Standing water in the yard

    Wait 24 to 36 hours, unless there is damage.

  • How often to clean gutters

    About twice a year: spring and early fall.

  • How deep to lay mulch

    2 to 3 inches, and never against the bark.

What yard work comes up through the year at a Maryland home?

Tall fescue lawns are aerated and seeded between mid-August and mid-October, and mowing can run from March to late November. Gutters need cleaning about twice a year. Mulch stays 2 to 3 inches deep, and standing water gets 24 to 36 hours to soak in unless it is causing damage. We take on these jobs around Annapolis.
