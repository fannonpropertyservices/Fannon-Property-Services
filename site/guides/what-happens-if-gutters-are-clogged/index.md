### What damage can clogged gutters do to a house?

Clogged gutters spill roof water at the wall, where a few inches of rain can mean several thousand gallons. The soil soaks, water can wick into the basement and lead to mold and rot, and in winter a gutter full of ice can add to an ice dam. We clean gutters around Annapolis, and estimates are free.

## Questions

### Can clogged gutters cause basement flooding?

They can. Roof water that spills at the wall soaks the soil around the foundation, and from there it can wick through into the basement or crawlspace.

### Can clogged gutters cause roof leaks?

Not on their own. Ice dams come from snow that melts by day and refreezes at night, and after several of those cycles water can work under the shingles into the attic. A gutter full of leaves and ice can add to a dam that is already forming, so clear gutters before winter.

### How do I keep ice dams from forming?

Clear leaves and sticks from the gutters and downspouts before winter, so melting snow can drain. Keep snow and icicles off them all winter. Good attic insulation, with air flowing under the roof from the eaves, also helps.

### Do clogged gutters attract pests?

Standing water does. Pooling water attracts a variety of pests, and leaves and plant debris built up in a gutter can invite animals to nest.
