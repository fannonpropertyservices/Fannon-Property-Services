## Mid-August to late November.

Seed goes down by mid-October at the latest. For sod, mid-October is the date to aim for.

*Aerate and seed: the middle of August to the middle of October; Clear leaves: the end of October to the middle of November*

- About 2 weeks for grass seed to sprout. November frosts can damage young seedlings.
- ½ inch shorter on the final mow makes snow mold less likely.

### What yard work should I do in the fall in Maryland?

Aerate and seed by mid-October, clean the gutters, plant shrubs, clear thick leaf cover and mow until the grass stops growing. Grass seed takes about 2 weeks to sprout. Leaf removal falls in late October to mid-November. The last mow may come in late November.

## Questions

### When do fall cleanups start in Maryland?

Late October, if the job is leaves. In Maryland, leaf removal falls in late October to mid-November, and only where leaves thickly cover the lawn. Seeding comes sooner, with mid-October as the last date for seed.

### What can I plant in September in Maryland?

Grass seed and shrubs. September sits inside the mid-August to mid-October window for seeding a tall fescue lawn. Fall is also a good time to plant trees and shrubs. The earlier in fall they go in, the longer they have to take root before winter.

### Does Anne Arundel County pick up bagged leaves?

Yes, for most households, at the curb all year, in paper yard waste bags or a rigid can marked with an X. Our yard waste pickup rules guide has the weight limit, bundle sizes and the City of Annapolis rules.
