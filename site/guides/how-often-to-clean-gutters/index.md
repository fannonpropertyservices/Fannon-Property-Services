When to clean gutters

## Spring and early fall.

A clogged gutter can overflow and pour water along the foundation.

*Spring: the start of March to the start of June; Early fall: the start of September to the middle of October*

### How many times a year do gutters need cleaning?

Gutters should be cleaned about twice a year, in spring and again in early fall, and any time they clog. With the debris cleared out, rain can flow down the downspout and away from the house. A clogged gutter can overflow and pour water in front of a basement window or along the foundation.

Sources: Delaware Department of Natural Resources and Environmental Control and University of California Agriculture and Natural Resources

## What to check.

- **Clear the debris** Rain needs a clear path to the downspout.
- **Check the downspouts** They should point and drain away from the house.
- **Trees around the house?** Consider gutter guards to keep leaves from clogging the gutters.
- **Guards need checks too** Guards can help keep debris out, but may still need attention.

## Questions

### When should gutters be cleaned in the fall?

Early fall. Clean them out again whenever they clog, so if leaves block the gutters later in the season, clear them once more.

### Do gutters need to be cleaned every year?

Yes, about twice a year, and more often where severe storms are common. Regular upkeep also helps keep gutters from corroding and pulling loose.

### What happens if you don't clean your gutters before winter?

Water backs up and overflows in fall, and melting snow can freeze into ice dams in winter. The guide What clogged gutters cause, linked above, follows each path the water takes.

### Should I clean my own gutters or hire someone?

Either can work. If you do it yourself, set a sturdy ladder on level ground. Never crawl onto the roof, and never work when storms or ice are possible. If you would rather stay on the ground, we clean gutters, and the estimate is free.

### Is gutter cleaning necessary?

Yes. Mosquitoes breed in standing water, and as little as one teaspoon is enough. The guide What clogged gutters cause, linked above, shows the rest of the damage.
