## Lawn.

- ![A walk-behind core aerator on a lawn, with soil plugs on the grass behind it](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/gen/services/aeration-phone.webp) [When to aerate and overseed](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/when-to-aerate-and-overseed/) Mid-August to mid-October, for tall fescue.
- ![A landscaper pushing a mower across a striped front lawn](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/gen/services/mowing-phone.webp) [Maryland lawn calendar](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/maryland-lawn-calendar/) Mowing can run March to late November.
- ![Drawing of tall fescue growing in three dense clumps with roots fanning under each](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/drawn/gs-tall-fescue.svg) [Grass seed for a Maryland lawn](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/best-grass-seed-for-maryland/) Turf-type tall fescue, at least 90% of the mix, with a fresh tag.
- ![Drawing of a tree with a wide flat ring of mulch and sedges under it where the lawn stops](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/drawn/sh-mulch-ring.svg) [Grass won't grow in shade](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/grass-wont-grow-in-shade/) 4 hours of sun for tall fescue, 2 for fine fescue. Under that, mulch.
- ![Drawing of a stiff wire pushed a foot into lawn soil beside a ruler, standing straight](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/drawn/cs-wire-test.svg) [Hard or compacted soil](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/hard-or-compacted-soil/) A wire that will not go in a foot means packed soil. Aerate in fall.
- ![Drawing of two tufts of grass, one cut clean and ticked, one with frayed brown tips and crossed out](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/drawn/mh-blade-tips.svg) [Mowing height in Maryland](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/mowing-height-maryland/) 3 to 4 inches for fescue, lower for bluegrass, lowest for zoysia.
- ![Drawing of a carpet of grass seedlings an inch tall, with a shoe print beside them crossed out](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/drawn/ns-week3.svg) [Watering and mowing new grass](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/new-grass-seed-watering-and-first-mow/) Light water several times a day until the first mow at 4 to 6 weeks.
- ![Drawing of a lawn seen from above with a worn path, a tree's shadow, a dog spot and two mower ruts, each named](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/drawn/tl-causes.svg) [Thin or patchy lawn](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/thin-or-patchy-lawn/) Fescue cannot close its own gaps. Fix the cause and overseed in fall.

## Wet ground, gutters and beds.

- ![A worker on a ladder lifting leaves out of a gutter in autumn](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/gen/services/gutter-cleaning-phone.webp) [How often to clean gutters](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/how-often-to-clean-gutters/) About twice a year: spring and early fall.
- ![A wide puddle on a lawn beside a downspout](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/gen/services/standing-water-phone.webp) [Standing water in the yard](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/standing-water-in-yard/) Wait 24 to 36 hours, unless there is damage.
- ![Drawing of a roof edge with a gutter packed with leaves, rain water spilling over its front and landing against the house wall](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/drawn/clog-overflow.svg) [What clogged gutters cause](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/what-happens-if-gutters-are-clogged/) Water at the wall, in the basement and in the ice at the eave.
- ![Drawing of a downspout turning into an extension pipe that runs out along a lawn sloping away from the house, with a splash block at its end](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/drawn/dsp-extension.svg) [Where downspouts should drain](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/where-should-downspouts-drain/) At least 5 feet out, onto ground that slopes away.
- ![Drawing of a gutter cut open, leaves jammed where it meets the downspout and water spilling over the far end](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/drawn/go-outlet-clog.svg) [Gutters overflowing in heavy rain](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/gutters-overflowing-in-heavy-rain/) A blocked outlet, a run with no slope or a gutter too small.
- ![A gutter cleared of leaves, seen from the ladder](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/own/portfolio/img_0439.webp) [Clean gutters yourself or hire someone](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/clean-gutters-yourself-or-hire/) One story, firm ground, a sound ladder: do it. Higher: hire it.
- ![A brick-edged flower bed in bloom beside a mown lawn](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/own/portfolio/img_0581.webp) [How deep to lay mulch](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/how-deep-should-mulch-be/) 2 to 3 inches, and never against the bark.
- ![Drawing of black-eyed Susan stems left standing in snow with their dark seed heads, a small bird perched on one](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/assets/drawn/cb-susan-standing.svg) [Cut back perennials in fall or spring](https://fannonpropertyservices.com/guides/cut-back-perennials-fall-or-spring/) Spring, for healthy plants. Only diseased stems come down in fall.

### What yard work comes up through the year at a Maryland home?

Tall fescue lawns are aerated and seeded between mid-August and mid-October, and mowing can run from March to late November. Gutters need cleaning about twice a year. Mulch stays 2 to 3 inches deep, and standing water gets 24 to 36 hours to soak in unless it is causing damage. We take on these jobs around Annapolis.
