## How far from the foundation

5 feet

At least, where the water comes out on open ground.

*Drawing of a house seen from above with a downspout at each corner, each running out toward rings marked 5 feet and 10 feet from the walls*

- **10 feet** from the property line is where Anne Arundel County suggests ending one.
- **6 inches** of fall over the first 10 feet keeps water moving away.

### How far should downspouts extend from the house?

At least 5 feet from the foundation, letting the water out onto ground that slopes away from the house. A buried pipe should carry it 10 feet or more, to a dry well or other catchment. The ground itself should fall half an inch per foot for 10 feet. We clean gutters around Annapolis, so water reaches the downspout.

## Questions

### Are splash blocks enough by themselves?

Not right at the wall, where they let the water out too close to the house. At the end of an extension that reaches 5 feet, a splash block helps keep the soil from washing away.

### Can downspouts be piped underground?

Yes. A solid, unperforated pipe can carry the water at least 10 feet away and downhill, to a dry well or an outlet at the surface. Never join it to the perforated foundation drain, which soaks the foundation. In Anne Arundel County, never to the storm drain.

### Can a downspout drain into a rain garden?

Yes. Anne Arundel County names rain gardens as one place to send it. A rain garden can let about 30% more water soak in than the same patch of lawn, and it belongs more than 10 feet from the house. A new one may qualify for the county tax credit.

### Is there a county tax credit for stormwater work?

Yes, for some. 10% of the cost of a qualified stormwater practice comes off county property tax each year for 5 years, up to $10,000 in all. The practice must be on the county's approved list, be your own choice rather than a requirement, and pass a county inspection; you apply within 45 days after the work ends, and the credit starts the tax year after approval.

### Can I point a downspout at my neighbor's yard?

It should not go there. In Anne Arundel County the water should drain onto your own property, not onto the property next door or county land. Ending it at least 10 feet from the property line lets it soak in first.
